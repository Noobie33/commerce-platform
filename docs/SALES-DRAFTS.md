# Sales draft foundation

> **Scope update - 9 November 2026:** The [retail-only master plan](RETAIL-ONLY-MASTER-PLAN.md) is authoritative. Wholesale/B2B and customer trade credit are excluded from the target system. Existing implementation details below remain accurate until the planned forward migration; this documentation change does not remove features or alter business data.

## Implemented boundary

Sales is a separate feature module. Staff with global `sales.read` can search saved sales orders and look up active customers, locations and active sellable variants at their catalog prices. This permission explicitly includes order contact/address snapshots and sales customer lookup; it does not grant customer-profile editing, catalog editing or physical inventory balances. Global `sales.manage` plus read permits creating/editing/cancelling drafts. Location-only grants do not authorize this shared commercial workspace. Stock/fulfillment scope will be enforced separately when those workflows arrive.

The first sales increment supports retail/wholesale channels, versioned customer selection, a fulfillment location, an optional requested date/reference/notes, up to 30 distinct variants, exact integer quantities and BDT goods totals. An active customer must support the selected channel; a customer may support both. Businesses and people use the same identity records. Customer and product details are snapshotted at save. Reading a saved draft does not silently substitute changed customer contacts or product names. Explicit editing/save refreshes the snapshots and checks the customer version.

This is preparation only. Saving a draft does not automatically reserve stock. [Explicit timed stock holds](SALES-RESERVATIONS.md) now reserve/release quantities separately; an active hold must be released before draft edit/cancel. Draft preparation still does not deduct physical stock, approve credit, post revenue, create receivables, record payments or calculate available credit. The UI states this at entry, review and detail. No confirmation, dispatch, invoice or payment button is presented before the corresponding invariants exist. The total is goods only; taxes, delivery charges, discounts, price lists and promotions are later workflows. The requested date is not a shipping commitment. Customer-facing confirmations or notifications are not sent.

## Prices and reasons

Catalog-price mode compares the submitted reviewed price with the current catalog price under a shared row lock. A changed catalog price fails with an explicit refresh requirement rather than silently repricing an order. `Refresh catalog prices` reloads the selected variants and updates catalog-mode lines; it keeps deliberate override prices. An unavailable product must be removed/replaced.

Override mode always requires separate global `sales.price_override` and a non-empty order-level reason, even when the amount happens to equal catalog price. The draft retains the comparison catalog price, selected unit price/mode and current override reason. Without override permission an existing override can be reset to catalog mode and refreshed before saving. Wholesale is a sales channel, not an automatic percentage discount. Drafts remain editable; an immutable quote/issued-document price revision workflow has not yet been implemented.

Money is integer minor units in PostgreSQL and BigInt calculations at validation/review boundaries. Prices accept up to two decimal places; no floats, exponent syntax or rounding are used. Per-unit maximum is 999,999,999,999 minor units and order maximum 99,999,999,999,999 minor units. Quantity is a positive whole number up to 999,999,999. Totals are recomputed from validated lines; clients cannot assign totals/status/numbers. Duplicate variants are rejected, not silently merged.

## Transactions and retention

Migration 11 adds sales orders, snapshotted lines, an owner-only numbering sequence and a dedicated idempotency namespace. Runtime receives SELECT and EXECUTE on one narrow SECURITY DEFINER function with fixed `pg_catalog` search path and qualified application tables. It has no direct DML or sequence access. The function checks fresh active/onboarded global read/manage/override permissions before cached replay, locks request keys and order revisions, locks active customer/location/catalog rows while validating, and commits header, line replacement, audit and minimal retry response atomically. Failed attempts consume no retry record; sequence gaps are expected. Unchanged successful retry returns the same `{id,version,kind}`, without another PII copy in general audit/cache.

Cancellation requires a reason and expected version. It preserves number, snapshots, lines and total, records actor/time through the transaction/audit, and prevents further edits. There is no delete endpoint. Migration rollback refuses to discard any sales orders. Use a reviewed forward migration or a complete tested backup restore for recovery, never delete posted history to force a downgrade.

`npm run sales:verify-local` reads counts, line/total consistency, required override reason, snapshot identity, document numbering and minimal retry result shape. It prints no customer identities or contact information. Development receives no sample orders. Browser QA uses a separate temporary staff account and only `commerce_platform_test`; cleanup removes owned sales requests/lines/orders before their customer/catalog fixtures.

## Verification and next acceptance gate

Real PostgreSQL/HTTP checks cover protected bounded lookup, exact multi-line totals, duplicate/concurrent requests, independent override authorization, CSRF, strict inputs/overflow/calendar dates, unavailable or incompatible records, customer/catalog changes, preserved snapshots, concurrent edit/cancel, audit-failure rollback for create/update/cancel, read-only runtime, fresh permission revocation, literal search/pagination, unchanged stock/payables and compiled migration apply/revert/reapply in an isolated schema. Unit checks cover canonical exact amounts, dates and strict input/query boundaries.

Timed reservations and available-stock enforcement are now implemented; the next sales increment must connect approval/confirmation and receivables. Available stock must account for reservations across every stock-decreasing path, with deterministic locks and final-unit race tests. Customer credit exposure must account for both outstanding invoices and confirmed uninvoiced credit commitments, without double counting. Confirmation must recheck the active customer, current credit policy, overdue rules and available credit under a customer lock; configured limits alone cannot authorize credit. Dispatch/invoice/payment/return/cancellation then need separate permissions, source-linked ledger records, immutable snapshots, exact allocations, audited reversals and reconciliation. These are acceptance requirements, not claims of existing functionality. Production deployment, backup/restore rehearsal and operational security remain separate outstanding work.

Browser QA verified customer/SKU lookup, wholesale preparation with two variants, exact BDT 6,400.45 total including an authorized override, requested date, review/save, catalog refresh retaining the override, edit and controlled cancellation with preserved lines/totals. Review focuses and scrolls within its dialog. The directory/form/table fit 390px; navigation and directory also fit 1024px and 1280px without page overflow. As the module list grew, medium desktop navigation was arranged into a brand/account row and a wrapping menu row. The preview contains test-only data; all fixtures were removed afterwards.
